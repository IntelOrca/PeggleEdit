Holes (called Teleports in older versions of PeggleEdit) are objects which move the ball to another part of the level or remove it completely to end the go.

## Appearance ##
If holes are visible, they will be displayed as a white box. Normally you will want to make the hole invisible and place a foreground image over the top using a poly or ball. PopCap's levels typically place a poly showing an image of a vortex which rotates.
