# Holes

Holes take the ball in and send it out somewhere else in the level, or take it away to end the shot. They were called Teleports in older versions of PeggleEdit.

With the **Select** tool, drag a selected hole's destination to move where the ball comes out.

## Hole settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Score | The points scored when the ball goes in. |
| Out Delay | How long the ball stays in the hole before it comes out. |
| Circular | Makes the hole round instead of rectangular. |

## Making holes look good
A hole with no image is drawn as a plain box. You can set the hole's own **Image**, which hangs down from the top of the hole. Or make the hole invisible and place an image over it with a [poly](Polys.md) or [ball](Balls.md). PopCap's levels often use a spinning vortex image this way.
