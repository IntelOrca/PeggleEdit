# Lines

Lines are thin walls between two points that the ball bounces off. They were called Rods in older versions of PeggleEdit.

With the **Select** tool, drag either end of a selected line to move it. You can also type the positions in **Point A, X**, **Point A, Y**, **Point B, X** and **Point B, Y**.

Lines cannot show an image. If you want a wall with a picture, use a thin [poly](Polys.md) instead.
